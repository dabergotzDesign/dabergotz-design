04/ Research

A Visual Language Built Around Humanity, Respect, and Elegance

faces upon faces

Research into obituaries and biography platforms helped identify which features could support meaningful interaction. Ideas like “On This Day” and obituary comments stood out, while others were intentionally avoided to maintain a respectful experience.

Obituaries and learning from medieval age

Exploring traditional obituaries helped define the project’s initial visual direction. Starting with monochrome palettes and subtle accents, I introduced lavender, inspired by its historical connection to mourning, as a defining element of the visual identity.

A project for the future

The final direction focused on three principles: human, respectful, and elegant. A monochrome palette with subtle lavender accents created the foundation, while Polaroid-style imagery and photo collages placed people at the heart of the experience. Quill & Legacy-inspired details added a refined visual touch.

05/ Decisions

Pushing Creative Ideas Within Technical Constraints

Bring the elements together

When I joined the project, several pages were incomplete or empty. I first restructured existing layouts, researched missing content, and refined the overall direction. Building on this foundation, I introduced new sections, designed additional pages, and added features to strengthen the experience.

What can we do with wordpress?

The project required constant balancing between design ambition and WordPress’s technical limitations. By moving between Figma and the CMS, I could validate ideas early and adapt designs to what was realistically achievable. Future iterations could push the experience further with animations, richer interactions, and shader-based effects.

06/ Visuals

Reimagining the Visual Language of Obituaries Through Color and Typography

Use what’s given & learn from the past

Inspired by traditional obituaries, I evolved the initial black-and-white concept into a softer and more cohesive visual identity. After exploring several accent colors, lavender stood out for both its visual character and historical association with mourning.

The typography combines an elegant serif with a clean sans-serif, complemented by a handwritten display font for a more personal touch.

07/ Reflection

The Journey Continues: Building on the Current Foundation

Artificial Intelligence or artificial team mate?

Google Stitch became a powerful tool for rapid exploration, generating multiple layout directions from the project’s style guide. This modular workflow made page creation faster and more iterative. However, AI-generated designs were only a starting point - components still needed careful refinement to achieve consistency with the established design system and visual identity.

a live prototype?!

To bridge the gap between Figma and a functional prototype, I tested several AI-assisted approaches, including Claude and Figma Make. FigmaToCode offered the most efficient workflow without an MCP, converting Figma frames into HTML and allowing me to quickly build and iterate on prototypes that closely reflected the intended final experience.

ongoing project

Remember Our Tales is a collaborative project developed by freelancers in their spare time. While progress is gradual, everyone involved continues to bring the vision closer to reality.
