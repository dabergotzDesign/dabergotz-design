about

Yep, that's me

Hello everyone, I'm Dennis Dabergotz, a UI & Web Designer with a background in Game Development and Front-End Web Development. My career began at the School for Games, where I studied Game Development and discovered my passion for creating digital experiences.

After transitioning into Front-End Web Development, I found myself increasingly drawn to the design side of the process-crafting intuitive interfaces, improving user experiences, and bringing ideas to life visually. This interest led me to expand my skills through UI/UX Design training, online courses, and continuous self-learning.

Today, I leverage both my technical and creative expertise to design engaging, user-focused websites and digital products.
