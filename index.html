Yep, that's me

When I’m not busy creating designs, you’ll probably find me rocking out at a concert, taking on a tough boss and slaying his minions in a game, or diving down a rabbit hole of code and interface experiments just for the fun of it. I enjoy tinkering with ideas, exploring new tools, and figuring out how things work behind the scenes — because curiosity doesn’t clock out when the workday ends.

And when I’m not chasing the next creative spark or virtual victory, I’m spending quality time with family and friends, recharging my batteries and enjoying the little things. Whether it’s through music, gaming, coding, or simply hanging out with the people I care about, I like to keep a healthy mix of creativity, adventure, and downtime in my life.
