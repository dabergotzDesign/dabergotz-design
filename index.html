ASOZIAL - SOCIAL MEDIA FOR THE ANTI-SOCIAL DEVELOPER

'asozial' noun: German for being anti-social.

Final project at Ironhack, I worked on the matching system for the social website asozial. The project’s style was based directly on the client’s existing website, ensuring a cohesive design. My main task was developing a Tinder-like matching system tailored for developers - think of it as Tinder meets LinkedIn, designed to help devs connect, collaborate, and network.

problem?

How can we effectively connect developers interested in working on projects with those offering them?

what used?

Figma, Affinity Designer, Photoshop

when?

Dec-Jan 2024/2025

what i did?

Research, Wireframing, Testing, Prototyping

Go Duck Yourself (Yes, We Mean Code)

A Rubber Duck Debugging App

Frustrated, you stare at your code as error messages pile up. You retreat to a quiet spot, explaining your code to a rubber duck - but it offers no feedback. What if an app could listen, encourage, and guide you forward?

problem?

How can we support the frustrated programmer/coder without intervene to much and let them solve their bugs.

what used?

Figma, Affinity Designer, Photoshop

when?

Jun-Jul 2025

Yep, that's me

Hello everyone, I'm Dennis Dabergotz, a Front-End Web Developer with a passion for design, games, and technology. Originating from a hands-on crafting background, I transitioned to the digital realm at the School for Games, initially aiming to become a Game Developer.

However, upon graduation, I was steered towards Web Development due to its stability compared to game development. I quickly grew fond of Web Development, particularly the Front-End and Design aspects. This led me to explore UI/UX Design through bootcamps, online courses and self-study. Now, I'm keen to pursue a career as a UI/UX Designer.

=======

Yep, that's me

When I’m not busy creating designs, you’ll probably find me rocking out at a concert, taking on a tough boss and slaying his minions in a game, or diving down a rabbit hole of code and interface experiments just for the fun of it. I enjoy tinkering with ideas, exploring new tools, and figuring out how things work behind the scenes — because curiosity doesn’t clock out when the workday ends.

Whether it’s through music, gaming, coding, or simply hanging out with the people I care about, I like to keep a healthy mix of creativity, adventure, and downtime in my life.
